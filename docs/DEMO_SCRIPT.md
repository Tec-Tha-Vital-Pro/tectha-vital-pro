# Client meeting demo script (25 minutes)

**Goal:** the owner agrees to a 30–45 day paid pilot at one branch.

## Before the meeting

- [ ] Run `python -m app.seed` so the data looks fresh, then start the app.
- [ ] Settings: type the client's hospital name and branch names.
- [ ] Open these tabs: Dashboard, Messages, Billing, Claims.
- [ ] Keep the clickable demo page open as a backup in case the laptop or internet fails.
- [ ] Optional: live mode with Meta's free test number and the owner's phone verified, so a real WhatsApp arrives on their phone.

## 1. Their problem, in their words (5 min)

Ask before showing anything:
- "How many enquiries came in last month, and how many became patients?"
- "How much is pending in dues right now?"
- "How do you hear about yesterday's numbers in the morning?"

Note their answers. You will use them in the proposal.

## 2. The owner's morning (3 min) — Dashboard

- Show both branches together, then switch to one.
- Point at "Needs attention": stuck claims, possible unbilled services, missed calls.
- "This arrives on your WhatsApp at 9 am every day. Nobody compiles Excel."

## 3. The patient's experience (5 min) — Messages

- Settings → **Run automations now**. Reminders, dues and feedback messages appear on the Messages screen.
- Simulate a patient: reply `1` to a reminder → the appointment shows Confirmed.
- Reply `2` to a feedback request → a complaint task appears for the manager.
- Message from a new number → a lead appears in the front-desk inbox instantly.

## 4. Money (5 min) — Billing and Claims

- Pending dues: press **Send reminder now** and show the WhatsApp text with a payment link.
- Unbilled services: "These were done in the lab or ward but are not on any bill." Press **Add to bill**.
- Claims board: red cards are past the insurer's 1-hour or 3-hour limit.

## 5. Trust (3 min)

- Your billing software stays. We connect to it; we don't replace it.
- Consent before any message. Every staff member has their own login. Every message is logged.
- WhatsApp account, server and data are in your hospital's name.
- Medical decisions stay with doctors.

## 6. The offer (4 min)

- **Pilot:** one branch, one problem (dues collection or appointment reminders), 30–45 days.
- **Success measure agreed today**, for example "no-shows from X% to Y%" or "₹Z of old dues collected".
- If it works, both branches, then the full sync and a monthly partnership.
- Ask: "Which problem should we fix first?"
