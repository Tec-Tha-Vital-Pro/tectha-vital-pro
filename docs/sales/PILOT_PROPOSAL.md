## 7. How we keep patient data safe

- **Consent first.** No message goes to a patient who has not agreed. Patients can reply STOP at any time.
- **Your accounts, your data.** WhatsApp, server and data are in your hospital's name. Tec Tha is only an administrator and can be removed at any time.
- **Stored in India.** The server is in an Indian region.
- **Own login for every staff member.** Each person sees only what their role and branch allow.
- **Everything is logged.** Every message and every change is recorded with who did it and when. Failed messages are shown, never hidden.
- **No medical advice.** The system handles communication and admin only. Clinical decisions stay with your doctors.
- **Written agreement.** An NDA and a data processing agreement are signed before we touch any patient data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
- **When we part ways,** we hand over a full copy of your data and remove our access.
